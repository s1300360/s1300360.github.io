About

Understanding people and organizations in an AI-enabled world.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Business Administration at Tamkang University. My work sits at the intersection of organizational behavior, technology management, and human-centered research on emerging technologies.

A recurring question in my research is how people and organizations respond when technology changes the meaning, structure, or constraints of work. My current projects examine AI-related career events, dignity and professional roles, bricolage and innovation, motivation to lead in human-AI systems, green AI, and dynamic feedback processes.

Methodologically, I combine survey research, experiments, qualitative inquiry, text analysis, and system dynamics to study organizational phenomena from multiple levels and time horizons.
